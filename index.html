How electricity costs are calculated

Electricity consumption is measured in kilowatt-hours (kWh). Multiply appliance power in watts by daily usage hours, divide by 1000, and multiply by your electricity price.

Daily cost = (Watts × Hours ÷ 1000) × Price/kWh
