How WattMetrica works

Three simple steps to understand electricity expenses.

01

Enter appliance power

Find the power rating in watts on your appliance label.

02

Set usage and price

Enter daily operating hours and your electricity price per kWh.

03

Calculation formula

Daily cost = (Power in watts × Hours per day ÷ 1000) × Price per kWh
